03 / THE DESIGN PROCESS

From requirements
to working hardware.

A clear engineering path connects the initial requirements, the design decisions, and what happens on the bench.

  1. 01

    Define the right system.

    Requirements, operating constraints, and system architecture establish what the electronics need to do and how the design will be delivered.

    REQUIREMENTS → ARCHITECTURE
  2. 02

    Design. Build. Integrate.

    Circuits, layout, firmware, and software develop together. Regular updates keep decisions visible and your project moving.

    DESIGN → A WORKING PROTOTYPE
  3. 03
